# Pitlane — Changelog

Milestones only, newest first.

## November 2026

- **v1.9:** Missing-price flags (Needs $, Price TBD, Sold with no $) with filters on the Pending tab. Reused-RO warnings. App icon. Entry grid that fills the window.
- **v1.8.1:** Earlier recommendations appear as soon as a known VIN is typed.

## October 2026

- **v1.8:** Report cleanup and redesign.
- **v1.7:** Advisors can be renamed without breaking past reports.
- **v1.6:** VIN tracking for returning vehicles, with a one-click re-add of previously declined work, plus undo.
- **v1.5:** Sign-in (bcrypt), admin-managed accounts and multi-store support.
- Board bulk actions: multi-select, delete and move entries to a new date.

## September 2026

- **v1.3:** Declined labor-hours tracking. Catalog of common jobs that auto-fills labor and price.
- **v1.2:** Labor hours and pricing on every line. Report toggles for labor and price. Ages shown in days and hours.
- Planned the path to a standalone installer and multi-store use.

## August 2026

- Rebuilt as a desktop app (Flask + PyWebView + SQLite) and renamed **Pitlane**.
- Pending working list as its own tab, per-advisor pending reports, PDF fixes.

## April – July 2026

- First version, "Rack Attack": a browser tool that replaced the Excel daily report, with a Board, an Insights dashboard, advisor goals and month-to-date reports.
- Analyzed close rate by follow-up timing. Next-day follow-up came out best, and it was adopted into the department's process.
